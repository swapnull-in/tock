Privacy Policy for Tock

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Swapnil Godambe ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Tock mobile application (the "App") for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data. By downloading and using Tock, you agree to the terms outlined below.

1. No Data Collection or Transmission

Tock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or device IDs.
  • The App does not make any network connections and operates entirely offline.

2. Local Storage Only

All information the App uses—including timer states, user preferences, and focus statistics—is stored locally on your device's secure sandbox storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third parties.

3. No Third-Party Analytics, Ads, or SDKs

To ensure absolute privacy, Tock contains:

  • No advertising networks.
  • No analytics software (such as Google Analytics or Firebase).
  • No third-party tracking Software Development Kits (SDKs).

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is saved locally on your hardware, uninstalling the App from your Android device will permanently and completely delete all of your stored data, timer configurations, and focus statistics. We do not retain backups on any remote servers.

5. Third-Party Services (Google Play)

While Tock itself does not collect any data, the App is distributed via the Google Play Store. Google may collect certain standard telemetry data, such as app crash logs, device models, and operating system versions, to help maintain application performance. For more information, please review the Google Play Services Privacy Policy.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
